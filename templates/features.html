Real-Time Risk Scoring

Evaluates payments in milliseconds. Outputs calibrated probabilities [0.0 - 1.0] and categorizes transactions into four clear risk tiers: LOW, MEDIUM, HIGH, and CRITICAL.

Global Currency Normalization

Accepts any of 60+ ISO 4217 world currencies. Automatically queries live or cached exchange rates to normalize transaction amounts to USD for model scoring while preserving the original currency in records.

Flags transactions where sender balance is not reduced by the exact payment amount, or destination balance fails standard banking reconciliation math.
